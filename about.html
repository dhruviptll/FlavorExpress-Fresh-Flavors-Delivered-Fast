Our story

Born from a love of great food

We started FlavorExpress with one simple mission — connect people with the best meals in their city, fast.

Who we are

More than just delivery

We're a passionate team of foodies, engineers, and dreamers on a mission to make every meal memorable.

FlavorExpress was founded in 2024 with a simple yet ambitious goal — make great food accessible to everyone, everywhere. We started in Ahmedabad and have since expanded across 200+ cities in India.

We partner exclusively with verified, high-quality restaurant partners who share our passion for fresh ingredients and great taste. Every order is tracked in real time, every payment is secure, and every delivery is guaranteed on time.

👥 For everyone who believes food is more than fuel — it's an experience.
